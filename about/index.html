About Mission Meals

A good meal can be a lifeline.

Mission Meals began with a simple belief: people who have served others should not face difficult seasons alone.

Our story

From one conversation to a working mission.

Chef Tony Dulaney saw an opportunity to use what he knew best—creating quality meals—to serve veterans in his community. Together, Tony Dulaney and Caryn Stinson shaped that idea into Mission Meals.

For 17 consecutive weeks, the pilot program prepared and served chef-created meals. It demonstrated both the need and the value of providing food that feels thoughtful, personal, and worthy of the people receiving it.

When access to the original kitchen ended because of permitting limitations, the meals paused—but the mission did not. Mission Meals, Inc. was established as an independent Georgia nonprofit corporation and began the search for a compliant kitchen and permanent home.

Our mission

★
“To provide chef-created quality meals to veterans, first responders, and others facing times of stress, hardship, recovery or need.”

Leadership

Built by people who believe service should be personal.

Founder & President

Tony Dulaney

A professional chef with a background in military service, law enforcement, and 14 years in emergency medical services. Tony combines his culinary expertise with a lifetime of serving others, bringing firsthand understanding of the challenges faced by veterans and first responders to the Mission Meals mission.

Founder & Vice President

Caryn Stinson

An entrepreneur with experience in food service, business ownership, and operations. Raised as a U.S. Air Force military dependent on bases throughout the United States and overseas, Caryn brings a personal connection to military life, along with her experience in strategic planning and organizational leadership, to Mission Meals.
