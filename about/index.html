About Mission Meals

A good meal can be a lifeline.

Mission Meals began with a simple belief: people who have served others should not face difficult seasons alone.

Our story

From one conversation to a working mission.

Chef Tony Dulaney saw an opportunity to use what he knew best—creating quality meals—to serve veterans in his community. Together, Tony Dulaney and Caryn Stinson shaped that idea into Mission Meals.

For 17 consecutive weeks, the pilot program prepared and served chef-created meals. It demonstrated both the need and the value of providing food that feels thoughtful, personal, and worthy of the people receiving it.

When access to the original kitchen ended because of permitting limitations, the meals paused—but the mission did not. Mission Meals, Inc. was established as an independent Georgia nonprofit corporation and began the search for a compliant kitchen and permanent home.
