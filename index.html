More Than a Meal

We believe a thoughtfully prepared meal can provide more than nourishment.

It offers encouragement, eases one burden, and reminds someone that they are not facing life’s challenges alone.

◎

Our Mission

To provide chef-created quality meals to veterans and others facing times of stress, hardship, recovery or need.

How We Serve

★

Dignity

Every meal is prepared and presented with the respect every person deserves.

✦

Excellence

We believe people facing hardship deserve the same quality meal we would proudly serve our own family.

◈

Stewardship

Partnerships Make the Mission Possible

Mission Meals works alongside local organizations, businesses, churches, veterans groups, healthcare providers, and community leaders who share a commitment to caring for those in need.

Together, we can reach more people, remove more burdens, and ensure that no one has to face difficult seasons alone.
